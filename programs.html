Program 01 · Inspected, then energised

Electrical installation

The regulations decide the work: circuit protection, earthing, cable sizing and separation are not matters of preference. What varies is whether a contractor books the inspection or leaves it as somebody else's problem. We book it, correct anything it records, have it re-inspected, and hand you the report before the installation is used.

Program 02 · Tested, not assumed

Data and network cabling

Cabling that looks right and tests badly is the most common fault we meet, and it is invisible until a connection drops under load months later. Every run is tested and the results are handed over, because a cabling job without test results is an assertion. We also keep data separated from power properly rather than sharing a convenient route.

Program 03 · Supplied and commissioned

IT and communications equipment

We supply the hardware as well as installing it, which means the statutory two-year guarantee of conformity applies to what we sell you, alongside whatever the manufacturer offers. We will also say when existing equipment is worth keeping: replacing a switch that tests fine to tidy a quote is not an upgrade, it is a sale.

Program 04 · Findings in writing

Testing and remedial work

A failed inspection report is a list, not a verdict, and most items on it are straightforward once someone reads it properly. We work through the non-conformities, arrange the re-inspection and hand over the clean report. Where a fault is in the supply rather than the installation we say so, because that belongs to the network operator.
