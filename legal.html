The company works under the professional competence required for the trades concerned, held under [PROFESSIONAL COMPETENCE / ACCESS TO THE OCCUPATION]. IMPORTANT — MANDATORY INSPECTION: a new electrical installation, and an existing installation that has been substantially modified or extended, must be inspected by an inspection body accredited for the purpose and found compliant with the General Regulation on Electrical Installations before it is put into service. The company arranges that inspection as part of the work, hands the report to the client, and does not energise an installation for use that has not passed it; where an inspection records non-conformities, they are corrected and the installation is re-inspected. An installation placed in service without that report cannot lawfully be used and an insurer may decline a claim arising from it.
